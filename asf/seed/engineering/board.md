# Board

[← Engineering](README.md)

What comes next and what is in progress, across repos — *Next* in the order I want it, rank 1
on top; *In Progress* newest pull on top. The items stay in their repos; a card holds only
its item's ID linked to the item, the repo linked to the repo, the item's title and my
reason. Status and priority are read from the
items, never copied here. A pull puts a card into *In Progress*, a close takes it off; the
repo names resolve in [repos.md](repos.md).
